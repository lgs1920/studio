To install **LGS1920 Studio** as an application on your iOS device using Safari:

1. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an upward arrow) in the toolbar at the bottom of the screen.
2. Scroll through the Share menu and select **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Verify the app name and tap **Add** in the top-right corner to add it to your home screen.
4. The app icon will appear on your home screen for quick access.

After installation, Studio checks for new releases automatically. When an update is ready, confirm the update in the
Studio dialog; the application restarts after the update is complete.
