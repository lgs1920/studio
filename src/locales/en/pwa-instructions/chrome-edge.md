To install **LGS1920 Studio** as an application in your browser:

1. Look for the **install icon** (a plus sign or download arrow) in the address bar.
2. Click the install icon and follow the on-screen prompts to confirm installation.
3. The app will be added to your desktop or applications menu for easy access.

After installation, Studio checks for new releases automatically. When an update is ready, confirm the update in the
Studio dialog; the application restarts after the update is complete.
