To install **LGS1920 Studio** as an application on your Android device:

1. Tap the **menu** button (three dots) in the top-right corner of the browser.
2. Select **Add to Home screen** or **Install app** from the menu.
3. Confirm the app name and tap **Add** or **Install** to place the app icon on your home screen.
4. The app will be available from your home screen or app drawer.

After installation, Studio checks for new releases automatically. When an update is ready, confirm the update in the
Studio dialog; the application restarts after the update is complete.
