## Replay, HQ Video Export, Widgets, and Map Providers

### Replay and Video Export

- Flythrough has been renamed to Replay across the interface and recording workflow.
- Replay now supports a more complete camera tracking model, with passive, navigation, and dynamic positioning behaviors refined for smoother route following.
- Camera angle offsets, heading hysteresis, marker visibility adjustments, and replay preview behavior have been improved for more readable animations.
- Replay playback and video recording can stay synchronized, including crop handling, progress feedback, and cleanup when recording ends.
- A deferred high-quality export pipeline now renders replay videos with dedicated frame capture and overlay
  composition.
- Video recording controls now share consistent indicators and provide clearer progress and finalization feedback.
- A low 15 FPS video preset is available, with the low-resolution preset grid restored and capture startup made more reliable.

### Widgets and Scene Composition

- Widget grid positioning controls have been added, including configurable columns, rows, margins, snapping, and grid visibility.
- Widget selection and snapping are scoped more reliably, preventing unrelated widgets from moving together.
- Video widgets can snap to the edges and centers of other widgets on the same board, including center alignment when
  widgets do not touch.
- The editable video crop snaps to the composition bounds without center, grid, or peer-widget snapping.
- Credits widgets can now be anchored and scaled while preserving their intended position.
- Logo widget for scene composition and video exports.
- Background toggles and dynamic widget rendering are more reliable during editing and capture.
- A new dynamic Stats widget displays live journey data during Replay playback.

### Maps, Layers, and Journey Data

- Users can now configure personal Cesium Ion layers with their own Ion token.
- New base layers, terrain sources, IGN Spain layers, and the Re:Earth elevation provider have been added.
- Direct 3D Tiles layers are supported, and WMS layers are proxied through Cesium resources where required.
- With a personal Ion token, users can use Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles and Google Map layers.
- A new journey sample asset, Mont Blanc, is available.
- Journey statistics refresh more reliably after profile edits, with improved altitude smoothing, ascent trends, and activity-specific defaults.
- Too-close stop POIs are hidden during replay to keep the map readable.

### User Interface and Project Information

- Theme contrast, drawer headers, welcome flow, typography, and layer error feedback have been refined.
- On-map themes now use consistent text and icon colors across recording, Journey, Orbit, Panoramic, and crop-ratio toolbars.
- On-map disabled menu items now have transparent backgrounds and better contrast.
- Replay drawer tabs now display badges to make available settings and sections easier to discover.
- A license information tab has been added to the information panel.
- Open-source, map-provider, terrain-provider, and sponsor credits have been updated.
- Technical documentation has been centralized and dependencies have been refreshed.

### Fixes and Reliability

- Fixed HQ replay rendering, frame sampling, camera alignment, camera visibility, and export finalization issues.
- Fixed Replay camera focus synchronization.
- Fixed camera angle persistence when moving between replay clips and corrected position-mode handling in HQ exports.
- Fixed replay/video synchronization problems, stale startup state, recording delays, and progress toolbar behavior.
- Journey toolbar visibility is restored correctly after linked replay video editing.
- Fixed widget grid persistence, snap behavior, selection scope, input editing, and background toggle regressions.
- Fixed video crop size persistence across editing sessions and removed resize feedback caused by generic widget scaling.
- Fixed replay widget opacity to respect configured values and kept widget toolbars and menus above video widgets.
- Fixed video cropper cleanup and UI state after cancelling or completing an export.
- Fixed POI visibility and filtering behavior, including stop POIs that were too close to the current position.
- Fixed map-provider configuration, 3D Tiles load errors, WMS handling, terrain branding, and Cesium Ion token initialization.
- Fixed journey color import and sequencing, sample GPX metrics, ascent calculations, and journey statistics refresh timing.
- Fixed welcome startup conditions, font loading, logo rendering, and layer disclaimer loading.
- The app icons and favicon have been updated.
- Numerous minor UI, rendering, and stability issues have also been resolved.
- Fixed widgets replacement after a scene change
