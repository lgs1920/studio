## Replay Camera, HQ Rendering, and Interface Improvements

### Replay and Video Rendering

- Draft and HQ replay rendering now share a common render-mode contract, with consistent camera initialization and common behavior.
- Replay camera tracking and collision navigation have been stabilized for short and long captures, variable playback speeds, delayed calculations, and changing terrain visibility.
- Replay camera paths now apply a bounded, speed-dependent roll on tight turns with eased transitions.
- Camera focus is preserved when leaving replay.
- Camera pitch correction, terrain visibility, render cadence, trace cleanup, and scene restoration have been refined to prevent stale state and unnecessary redraws after playback.

### Compass, Notifications, and Usage Reporting

- Two new compass variants, Modern and Flat, are available in global compass settings and the compass widget editor. 
- The previous Rose Compass variant has been removed.
- Anonymous count events can be sent for visits, journey loads, Draft videos, and HQ exports without storing visitor identity.

### Reliability and Technical Improvements

- Replay camera and render-mode behavior now has broader focused unit and parity test coverage.
- Replay overlays, camera focus transitions, video progress, and capture cleanup are more reliable across Draft and HQ workflows.
