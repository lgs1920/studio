## Drone Camera Path Architecture

- Documented deterministic camera paths with absolute WGS84 heights.
- Added clip-to-clip interpolation for camera angle, pitch, height, target, and zoom continuity.
- Added animated and immediate `fly-to` positioning.
- Added variable-speed zoom, take-off, landing, and spiral trajectories with easing.
- Added directly editable 3D Bezier paths with preserved or tangent-following orientation.
- Documented the public and private method surface for the future runtime.
